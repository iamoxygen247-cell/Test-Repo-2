FAQ

Common questions.

Does Clockwise Pro install software on engineers' computers? ▾
No. Clockwise Pro works entirely through OAuth integrations with Slack, Jira, and GitHub. There is nothing to install on any machine. Engineers simply connect their accounts once and the AI does the rest in the cloud.
How is Clockwise Pro different from Toggl or Harvest? ▾
Toggl and Harvest require engineers to manually start and stop timers. Clockwise Pro is fully automatic — the AI infers time allocation from activity signals in the tools your team already uses. No timers, no manual entries, no nagging reminders.
Is my team's data private and secure? ▾
Yes. Clockwise Pro is SOC 2 Type II certified, GDPR compliant, and encrypts all data at rest (AES-256) and in transit (TLS 1.3). Individual activity data is only visible to the engineer themselves. Managers see aggregated team-level insights only.
Can I upgrade or downgrade my plan at any time? ▾
Absolutely. You can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel your plan at any time from the billing page. Upgrades take effect immediately and you're charged a pro-rated amount for the remainder of the billing period. Downgrades take effect at the next renewal date.
What happens when I exceed 15 seats on the Team plan? ▾
Once you exceed 15 seats on the Team plan, additional seats are billed at the Pro per-seat rate ($12/mo, or $9.60/mo annually). There are no sudden price cliffs — reach out to sales and we'll put together a custom enterprise quote if you're scaling fast.
Is there a free trial for the Pro or Team plans? ▾
Yes — both Pro and Team plans come with a 14-day free trial. No credit card required. After the trial you can continue with a free account or choose a paid plan. We'll send you a reminder 3 days before the trial ends.
