# Questions Asked + Assumed Answers

## Blockers

**Q1: What is the name of your design studio (or your own name if you work under your personal brand)?**
*Why it's a blocker:* The studio name is needed for the nav, hero headline, and footer. Without it, the page has no identity anchor.
*Assumed answer:* "Studio Forme" — a clean, minimal studio name that fits the modern/minimal aesthetic described.

---

## Important Gaps

**Q2: What is your name (the designer behind the studio)?**
*Why it matters:* Personal brand pages for freelancers often include a short "About" or "Meet the designer" section that builds trust with startup founders.
*Assumed answer:* "Alex Mercer" — used in the About section and footer byline.

**Q3: What are the 3–5 specific services you offer? (e.g., logo design, brand guidelines, Figma UI, design systems, etc.)**
*Why it matters:* The services grid is a key section — generic labels reduce credibility.
*Assumed answer:*
- Brand Identity (logos, color palettes, typography systems)
- UI/UX Design (wireframes, high-fidelity mockups, prototypes)
- Design Systems (component libraries, Figma tokens, documentation)

**Q4: Do you have a preferred accent/brand color, or should I use the default modern blue (#0071e3)?**
*Why it matters:* A design studio's page should feel intentional. A custom color signals craft.
*Assumed answer:* Using a warm near-black accent (#1a1a2e) for the primary brand color with an electric indigo (#5c5ce0) as the action/CTA accent — more distinctive than generic blue, fitting for a premium design studio targeting tech startups.

**Q5: Do you want to include a portfolio/work section, social proof (testimonials or client logos), or keep it purely conversion-focused?**
*Why it matters:* Shapes the page length and structure significantly.
*Assumed answer:* Include both a minimal "Selected Work" section (3 placeholder project cards) and 2–3 brief testimonials to build credibility. Startup founders need both inspiration and trust signals before booking a call.
